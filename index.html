M6 Triage

M6 Triage is a private assistant that runs on one computer for a small wedding-hosting business. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

Through a private Telegram chat, it helps the business owner keep a record of client inquiries in a Google Sheet it creates in her account, place date holds on her business calendar, and check those dates for clashes. Nothing is written to her Google account until she confirms it.

See the privacy policy for what it reads and stores.
