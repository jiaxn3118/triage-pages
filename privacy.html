M6 Triage privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

M6 Triage is a private assistant for one small wedding-hosting business. It runs on a single computer owned by its developer and is used only by the business owner and the developer. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses in Google

  • Google Sheets and Drive: only the spreadsheet the app itself creates (the inquiry record). It cannot see or open any other file in the account.
  • Google Calendar: events on calendars the account owns, so it can place, update and remove the date holds it created, and read existing events to check for clashes. It never changes or deletes an event it did not create, and it never sends invitations or emails to anyone.
  • Free/busy: busy or free times of a calendar shared with the account, without event details.
  • Basic account identity: a one-way fingerprint of the account, used only to make sure the app is connected to the right account.

How the information is used

Only to keep the business's inquiry record and calendar holds, and to answer the business owner's own questions about them. Nothing is written to the Google account unless the business owner confirms it first.

Where it is kept

  • Inquiry records and holds stay in the business owner's own Google account.
  • The sign-in token is kept in the computer's encrypted system keychain.
  • A daily backup copy of the inquiry record and a short activity log are kept on the same computer. Client messages pasted into the assistant are deleted from it after 30 days.
  • Text is understood by an AI model that runs on the same computer. No Google data is sent to any outside AI service.
  • Conversations with the assistant take place in a private Telegram chat, under Telegram's own terms.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account holder can remove the app's access at any time in their Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services. The developer can also disconnect it, which revokes the token and deletes it from the computer. Removing access does not delete the spreadsheet or calendar entries; they remain the account holder's to keep or delete. To ask for the local backup and logs to be deleted, contact the developer.
